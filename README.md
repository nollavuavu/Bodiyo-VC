# Bodiyo

Bodiyo is an evidence-based gym app prototype. Every training recommendation comes with a "Why?" backed by research on mechanical tension.

**Current version: `prototype-v2/`.** Open `prototype-v2/index.html` in a browser to run it. `prototype/` is the older v1, kept for reference.

## Folder layout

- `prototype-v2/`: the current app (HTML, CSS and JS), with its own README, design audit, tests in `tools/` and screenshots.
- `prototype/`: v1 of the app.
- `evidence.md`: the research library behind each "Why?" (15 topics, with citations).
- `exercises.json`: the exercise library (muscles, equipment, resistance profile, stretch position and swaps), with `exercises-README.md` and `exercises-citations.md`.
- `i18n/`: translations (English and Vietnamese) and the glossary.
- `qa-*.md`: QA reports, including the citation, numbers and contradiction audits.
- `qa-screens/`: QA screenshots.
- `security.md`: the security audit and the plan for accounts and subscriptions.
- `app-teardown.md` and `design-notes.md`: competitor research and design notes.
- `TEAM.md`: who worked on what.
