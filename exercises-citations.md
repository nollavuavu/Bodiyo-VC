## Contradictions (resolved by Vinci in qa-bobby-contradictions.md v2)
Contradictions are owned by Agent Vinci (the contradictions owner per TEAM.md). The full log is `qa-bobby-contradictions.md` (v2, Part B, Batch 1, items I1.1–I1.6), where all six items below are **resolved by Vinci in v2**. The list below was sent directly to Vinci on Oct 29, 2026 and was not investigated here. Only abstracts were read. After the v2 resolutions, the exercises named now carry notes from the papers above. Not used: Vinci's optional Pedrosa 2022 (leg-extension) and Plotkin 2023 (squat) lines, and the Pedrosa 2023 preacher line. `machine-hip-thrust`, `cable-curl` (Larsen 2026 removed per Stirner round 2: the trial's 0–90° descending-profile setup isn't this exercise), `dumbbell-curl` and the other calf raises stay empty.

1. Larsen 2025, PMID 40692697: dumbbell and cable lateral raises gave similar side-delt growth despite different resistance curves. This is in tension with the evidence.md topic 7/8 preference for stretch-loaded profiles. Applies to the lateral raises.
2. Kobayashi 2026 (PMID 42829982) found incline curls grew the biceps more than preacher curls, though total elbow-flexor growth was the same. Larsen 2026 (PMID 41988507) and Attarieh 2025 (PMID 40082069) found shoulder angle made no meaningful difference. Applies to incline, preacher, Bayesian and cable curls.
3. Gentil 2015 (PMID 26446291) found lat pulldowns grew the elbow flexors as much as curls. Mannarino 2021 (PMID 31268995) found rows grew them about half as much as curls. Applies to the lat pulldown, dumbbell curl and one-arm dumbbell row.
4. Pedrosa 2022 (PMID 33977835), Kassiano 2023 (PMID 37015016) and Pedrosa 2023 (PMID 36828324) found partials at long lengths beat other ranges. Wolf 2025 (PMID 39959841) and Gschneidner 2025 (PMID 41055237) found lengthened partials about equal to full range. Applies to the leg-press calf raise and the preacher curl. `leg-extension` uses only Kassiano 2026 (PMID 41379528), which isn't contested here.
5. Larsen 2025 (PMID 40113586) found knee range on the leg press didn't change quad growth. This is in tension with the topic 7 long-length claim. Applies to the leg press.
6. Plotkin 2023 (PMID 37877099) found hip thrusts, which are hardest at the top, grew the glutes about as much as back squats. This is in tension with topics 7/8. Applies to the hip thrusts. Crossref lists a 28 Oct 2026 correction covering conflict-of-interest, funding and trial-registration statements; the notice says the results are unaffected.
